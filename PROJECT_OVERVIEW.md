FOOTY vs HOOPS is a sports gaming and Web3 project built around a competitive rivalry between two of the world’s most popular sports: football and basketball.

* ⚽ **$FOOTY** — Football
* 🏀 **$HOOPS** — Basketball

The project’s central idea is to create an ecosystem where football and basketball compete to become the community’s favorite sport.

> **Main Slogan:** “Vote with your money for the best sport.”

The project combines sports culture, arcade gaming, community competition, digital collectibles, cryptocurrency concepts, and interactive entertainment.

The long-term objective is to create an internationally recognized sports gaming ecosystem with its own visual identity, community, game, and potential Web3 integrations.

## 2. The Core Concept

FOOTY vs HOOPS differs from conventional sports projects by putting two entire sports against each other rather than focusing on a single team, athlete, or league.

The community can support football or basketball, participate in the rivalry, play the game, customize characters, collect digital items, and follow the competition between the two sports.

The concept is designed to appeal to three overlapping audiences:
* **Football and basketball fans.**
* **Arcade and competitive multiplayer gamers.**
* **Crypto and Web3 communities.**

The project aims to make the rivalry between football and basketball the foundation for its game mechanics, community activities, content, and future ecosystem.

## 4. Gaming Product

FOOTY vs HOOPS includes a full-featured arcade sports game built around football and basketball gameplay.

Key features implemented in the current production product:
* **Two distinct sports modes:** ⚽ Football and 🏀 Basketball.
* **Playable characters and customization:** Footy (#10 Striker) and Hoops (#23 Forward).
* **10 Detailed Scenic Arenas:** Stadiums spanning from Coastal Sunset Court and Redwood Forest Arena to Orbital Space Colosseum and Neo Tokyo Megacity.
* **Extensive Wardrobe:** 68 cosmetic items across jerseys, match balls, celebration effects, trails, and frames.
* **Tactical Boosts:** 8 gameplay boosts (Super Shot, Overdrive, Magnet Shield, Coin Doubler, etc.).
* **In-Game Shop & Bundles:** 7 thematic collections with item breakdowns and multi-currency pricing.
* **Seasonal Battle Pass:** 30 tiers with parallel Free and VIP tracks.
* **Practice against AI:** 3 difficulty levels (Easy, Normal, Hard) with dynamic AI opponent logic.
* **Real-Time Online 1v1 Multiplayer:** WebRTC P2P netcode with native RFC6455 WebSocket relay signaling and multi-region STUN servers.
* **Private Match Rooms:** 6-digit room code system to challenge friends in real-time.
* **Mobile-Friendly Controls:** Dual touch virtual joystick, action buttons (Shoot, Turbo, Boost), full Telegram WebApp haptic and back-button integration.
* **Bilingual Localization:** Complete key parity between Russian and English.

## 5. In-Game Economy

The game uses an internal currency called **FvH Coin**, intended for in-game purchases and progression.

Uses of FvH Coin:
* Cosmetic items and jersey kits.
* Character equipment and balls.
* Stadium arena unlocks.
* Tactical boosts and visual trails.
* Curated bundles and seasonal content.

*Important distinction: FvH Coin is strictly an internal in-game soft currency. It is distinct from $FOOTY and $HOOPS and is not represented as a transferable cryptocurrency or real-money withdrawal mechanism.*
